The Linux kernel Bug Fixing mentorship program.

November 21, 2025

My experience as a mentee.

Introduction:

Hello folks, in this article I'll talk about my experience in the linux kernel mentorship program.

Application:

I found out about the program through linkedin. I applied through the linux foundation mentorship page on 2025 summer round. So, keep an eye out for when the program opens up again [1]. Also, it's a chance to explore other projects provided by the linux foundation. The program is led by Shuah Khan [2] .It's unpaid. and it clearly states that. It does not hold your hand. And you will gain insight and mentorship on contributing to the linux kernel code base. ![Screenshot from 2025-11-21 16-53-07.png](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/eslammedhat/eslammedhat.github.io/main/images/1763736819048-Screenshot from 2025-11-21 16-53-07.png)
